The story in four steps

  1. Complaints: what app users say is broken.
  2. Funnel: where web shoppers drop off.
  3. Customers: who drives spend, and whether campaigns and coupons work.
  4. A/B test: how we would test the top fixable problem.

Where the data comes from

  • App reviews: Google Play and App Store, Irish stores, Oct 2024 to Oct 2026. Self-selected, skew negative.
  • Web funnel: GA4 public sample, Google Merchandise Store. Real e-commerce, not grocery.
  • Customers: The Complete Journey (84.51° release), an anonymised US grocer in 2017.
  • None of it is Tesco data.

Test plan: one-tap wallet payment

  • Change: add Apple Pay / Google Pay to the payment page.
  • Why: payment complaints in reviews, and a real drop at the payment step.
  • Unit: user, 50/50, analysed once they reach the payment page.
  • Guardrails: order value, overall conversion, payment errors, page speed.
  • Rules: check the split first, no peeking, ship only if the main metric is up and no guardrail is harmed.
